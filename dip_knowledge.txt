DIP DIET - DISCIPLINED & INTELLIGENT PLAN
==========================================
A whole-food, plant-based nutrition system designed by Dr. Biswaroop Roy Chowdhury.

===================
CORE PHILOSOPHY
===================
The DIP Diet is based on natural, unprocessed, plant-based foods. It follows the principle that the human body heals itself when given the right nutrition in the right quantities at the right times.

===================
MEAL STRUCTURE
===================

--- BREAKFAST (Until 12:00 PM ONLY) ---
- Consume ONLY fresh, seasonal, whole fruits.
- No fruit juices, packaged fruits, or dried fruits.
- Minimum Fruit Quantity Formula: Body Weight (kg) x 10 = grams of fruit required.
  Example: A person weighing 70 kg must eat at least 700 grams of fresh fruits for breakfast.
- Recommended fruits: Papaya, Watermelon, Mango, Banana, Apple, Guava, Pear, Pomegranate, Orange, Grapes, Kiwi, Berries, Seasonal local fruits.
- You can mix different fruits to reach the minimum weight target.
- Breakfast must be completed before 12:00 PM noon.

--- LUNCH (12:00 PM - 4:00 PM recommended) ---
Lunch is a TWO-PLATE system:

  PLATE 1 (MANDATORY FIRST):
  - Raw, uncooked vegetables (salad).
  - Minimum Raw Vegetable Quantity Formula: Body Weight (kg) x 5 = grams of raw vegetables required.
    Example: A person weighing 70 kg must eat at least 350 grams of raw vegetables.
  - Recommended vegetables: Cucumber, Tomato, Radish (Mooli), Carrot, Capsicum, Lettuce, Beetroot, Cabbage, Onion, Spinach leaves.
  - You MUST finish all of Plate 1 before starting Plate 2.

  PLATE 2 (Only after Plate 1 is completely finished):
  - Home-cooked, plant-based meals.
  - Use MINIMAL oil (maximum 1 tsp per meal) and MINIMAL salt.
  - Allowed foods: Cooked vegetables, legumes (dal/lentils), beans, brown rice, whole wheat roti/chapati, millets (jowar, bajra, ragi), oats.
  - No dairy products in this plate.
  - No animal-based products.

--- DINNER (Must be completed STRICTLY before 8:00 PM) ---
Dinner follows the SAME TWO-PLATE system as lunch:

  PLATE 1 (MANDATORY FIRST):
  - Raw, uncooked vegetables (same formula: Body Weight x 5 grams).
  - Must be eaten completely before starting Plate 2.

  PLATE 2 (Only after Plate 1 is completely finished):
  - Light, home-cooked, plant-based meal.
  - Keep it lighter than lunch for better digestion.
  - Avoid heavy, oily, or fried foods at dinner.

CRITICAL DINNER RULE: Dinner MUST be completed before 8:00 PM. Eating after 8 PM is strictly prohibited on the DIP Diet.

===================
QUANTITY FORMULAS
===================
- Morning Fruits: Body Weight (kg) × 10 = minimum grams
  70 kg → 700 g fruits | 80 kg → 800 g fruits | 60 kg → 600 g fruits

- Raw Vegetables (per meal): Body Weight (kg) × 5 = minimum grams
  70 kg → 350 g raw veggies | 80 kg → 400 g raw veggies | 60 kg → 300 g raw veggies

===================
FOODS TO AVOID COMPLETELY
===================
1. All packed and processed foods (chips, biscuits, namkeen, ready meals)
2. Refined sugar and all products containing refined sugar (white sugar, corn syrup, glucose)
3. All dairy products: Cow milk, buffalo milk, cheese, paneer, butter, ghee, cream, yogurt/curd, ice cream
4. All animal products: Meat (chicken, mutton, pork, beef), fish, seafood, eggs
5. Refined grains: White rice (polished), maida/all-purpose flour, white bread, pasta made from maida
6. Cold drinks, sodas, energy drinks, packaged juices
7. Alcohol and tobacco products
8. Artificial sweeteners and food additives/preservatives
9. Deep fried foods: Samosa, puri, pakora, French fries
10. Fast food and restaurant junk food

===================
HEALTHY SUBSTITUTIONS
===================
- Replace cow/buffalo milk → Homemade almond milk or coconut milk
- Replace refined white sugar → Dates, jaggery (gud), honey (raw), ripe bananas as sweetener
- Replace white rice → Brown rice, millets (jowar/bajra/ragi), quinoa
- Replace maida → Whole wheat flour, besan (gram flour), ragi flour
- Replace butter/ghee → Small amount of cold-pressed coconut oil or completely skip
- Replace packaged snacks → Fresh fruits, soaked nuts, sprouts
- Replace tea/coffee with milk and sugar → Herbal teas, lemon water, coconut water
- Replace processed cheese → Homemade nut-based spreads (cashew cream)

===================
HYDRATION GUIDELINES
===================
- Drink 2-3 liters of plain water daily.
- Best times: First thing in the morning (2-3 glasses on empty stomach), between meals (NOT during meals).
- Avoid drinking water during meals as it dilutes digestive enzymes.
- Coconut water is allowed as a hydrating beverage.
- Herbal teas (tulsi, ginger, green tea without milk) are allowed.

===================
SAMPLE DAILY MEAL PLAN
===================

MORNING (7:00 AM - 12:00 PM):
- 2-3 glasses of plain water on waking up
- Fresh seasonal fruits (minimum Body Weight × 10 grams)
- Example for 70 kg person: 300g watermelon + 200g papaya + 200g banana = 700g total

LUNCH (12:30 PM - 1:30 PM):
- Plate 1: Mixed raw salad bowl (Cucumber + Tomato + Carrot + Radish = 350g for 70 kg)
- Plate 2 (after finishing Plate 1): 2 whole wheat rotis + 1 cup moong dal + cooked vegetables

EVENING SNACK (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM):
- Fresh fruits or soaked nuts (almonds, walnuts)
- Coconut water

DINNER (Before 8:00 PM):
- Plate 1: Mixed raw salad (350g for 70 kg person)
- Plate 2 (after finishing Plate 1): Light khichdi with vegetables or vegetable soup with millets

===================
SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR HEALTH CONDITIONS
===================

DIABETES:
- Strictly follow the DIP Diet protocol.
- Monitor blood sugar regularly.
- Consult your doctor before reducing diabetes medications as blood sugar will improve.
- Avoid all sugary fruits initially; focus on low-glycemic fruits like guava, berries, papaya.

HIGH BLOOD PRESSURE:
- Reduce salt intake as much as possible.
- Focus on potassium-rich fruits (banana, watermelon, coconut water).
- Avoid pickles, papad, namkeen completely.

WEIGHT LOSS:
- Follow all DIP rules strictly.
- Do not skip breakfast fruits — they are essential.
- Limit Plate 2 portions but never skip Plate 1 raw vegetables.

THYROID:
- Avoid raw cruciferous vegetables (cabbage, broccoli, cauliflower) in excess if hypothyroid.
- Prefer cooked cruciferous vegetables.

===================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
===================

Q: Can I eat fruits after 12 PM?
A: Ideally no. Fruits are best consumed in the morning. After 12 PM, follow the two-plate lunch system.

Q: What if I'm still hungry after following the diet?
A: Eat more fruits in the morning and more raw vegetables in Plate 1. There is NO upper limit, only a MINIMUM.

Q: Can I cook with oil?
A: Minimum oil only — maximum 1 teaspoon of cold-pressed oil per meal. Best to use as little as possible.

Q: Is paneer (Indian cottage cheese) allowed?
A: No. Paneer is a dairy product and is NOT allowed on the DIP Diet.

Q: Can I have tea or coffee?
A: Avoid tea and coffee with milk and sugar. You may have herbal teas without milk and sugar.

Q: How long before I see results?
A: Many people notice improvements in energy and blood sugar within 7-14 days of strictly following the DIP Diet.

Q: Can children follow the DIP Diet?
A: Yes, children can follow the DIP Diet. Adjust quantities based on their body weight using the same formulas.
